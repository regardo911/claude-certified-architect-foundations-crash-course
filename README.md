# Claude Certified Architect Foundations - Crash Course: Printable Sheets

**By exam morning you hold your cram sheet, a Top-20 card with your own five on it, a scored mock with a verdict, and a ritual card for the room.**

The companion to the book *Claude Certified Architect Foundations - Crash Course* from [youcanbuildthings.com](https://youcanbuildthings.com).

These are the book's sheets as blank pages, ready to print or copy, so you spend your weekend filling them in instead of ruling lines. Two terms come up first. Your *cram sheet* starts in Chapter 2 and gets one block per domain in Chapters 4 to 8. Your *Top-20 card* is Chapter 9's one-page squeeze of it, the page you skim on exam morning. Pick the branch below that matches how much time you have.

## Start here

### My exam is in a few days, or this weekend

Print the [front page](chapters/02-03-front-page/front-page.md) ([PDF](chapters/02-03-front-page/front-page.pdf)) and fill in the facts card and the weight map as you read Chapter 2.

### It's tonight

Print [Your last 24 hours](chapters/11-exam-day/last-24-hours.md) ([PDF](chapters/11-exam-day/last-24-hours.pdf)) and the [ritual card](chapters/11-exam-day/ritual-card.md) ([PDF](chapters/11-exam-day/ritual-card.pdf)). Appendix A wrote the checklist for exactly this night.

### I'm about to sit the mock

Print the [answer sheet](chapters/10-mock/answer-sheet.md) ([PDF](chapters/10-mock/answer-sheet.pdf)) and the [verdict page](chapters/10-mock/verdict.md) ([PDF](chapters/10-mock/verdict.pdf)). The 60 questions and the key are in Chapter 10 of the book.

Every sheet has a PDF beside it with the same name; open it and print. To edit one on screen instead, open the `.md`, click **Copy raw file**, and paste it into a single note. For everything at once, use the green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**.

Print, don't type, if you can. Appendix A says writing it by hand "is half the memorizing."
