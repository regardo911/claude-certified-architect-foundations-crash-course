# Book fixes

The printed book can't change. Where two of its passages disagree about a sheet, this repo picks one, and says so here.

## Chapter 2 vs Chapters 8 and 9: one page or several

Chapter 2: "The whole book is engineered to fill exactly one page, no more". Chapter 1: "The thing you'll build as you read is one page."

Chapter 8: "it's grown to several pages". Chapter 9: "Your cram sheet is now several pages deep. Time to crush it to one."

The sheets follow Chapters 8 and 9. The cram sheet is a front page plus one page per domain block, and the Top-20 card is the one page.

## Chapter 2 vs Appendix A: weight map or attack order

Chapter 2: "Then draw the **domain-weight map**, by name, biggest to smallest". Appendix A heads its list "The ranked attack order" and says it "replaces a flat weight chart".

The front page carries both: the weight map Chapter 2 has you draw, and under it the attack order Appendix A uses.

## Chapter 9 vs Chapter 10: how long the mock takes

Chapter 9's hour plans: "a final hour on the mock plus building your five" and "leave forty-five minutes for the mock". Chapter 10: "Sixty questions, a hundred and twenty minutes, no notes".

The answer sheet follows Chapter 10: 120 minutes.
