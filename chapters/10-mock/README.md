# Chapter 10: the mock

Chapter 10 of the book holds the 60 questions, and the answer key comes right after them. This folder holds the paper you mark them on.

## Before the clock starts

Print one copy of [answer-sheet.pdf](answer-sheet.pdf) and one of [verdict.pdf](verdict.pdf). Write your start time on the answer sheet, then go. 120 minutes, no notes. Don't open the key until all 60 have a letter next to them.

## Marking

Check each letter against the key, write right or wrong beside it, and count the misses in each of the five domain groups. The groups follow the book's mock order: questions 1 to 16, 17 to 28, 29 to 40, 41 to 51, 52 to 60. Your total out of 60 is a readiness signal, not a scaled score. The verdict page quotes the book on that.

## Where the misses go

Mark each miss under its domain on the answer sheet. Work your misses into [my-5.md](../09-top-20-card/my-5.md), up to five. If one domain is full of them, name it under "Fix these first" on the verdict page.

## The verdict

Fill in [verdict.md](verdict.md) last. It's done when it reads "you're ready when…", not "you scored X, book it." That's the book's own test.

If a domain lit up red, fix it first. Chapter 10 calls that "worth a day's delay over a wasted attempt."
