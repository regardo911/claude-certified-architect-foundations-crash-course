# Chapters 4 to 8: the five domain blocks

Five pages, one per domain, in chapter order. Each has a space for every BUILD STEP item and a line for your recall-check score.

## Print

One copy of [domain-blocks.pdf](domain-blocks.pdf). It comes out as 5 pages; staple them behind the front page and that stack is your cram sheet. Fill one page at the end of each chapter.

## Done when

| Chapter | Its checkpoint |
|---|---|
| 4 | "You can answer all five cold, and you get the batch-versus-loop call right without hesitating." |
| 5 | "You can reproduce the CLAUDE.md precedence order *and* the escalation protocol from memory" |
| 6 | "You classify all five correctly, and you can state the structured-output guarantee and its two exceptions without hedging." |
| 7 | "You pick the right transport for any deployment cold, and you never, ever pick standalone SSE." |
| 8 | "You can name the silent-failure modes and recite "escalate on policy, not vibes" cold" |

After the mock, a domain that keeps costing you points is the block to rebuild first. Appendix A puts it plainly: "a lit-up domain means rebuild that block first, even if it costs a day."
