# Chapter 9: the Top-20 card

Two sheets. [top-20-card.md](top-20-card.md) is the one page your cram sheet gets crushed into: twenty numbered lines in the ranked attack order and the "My 5" box. [my-5.md](my-5.md) is where you work each mock miss down to the one line that goes in that box.

Print two copies of [top-20-card.pdf](top-20-card.pdf), because Chapter 9 has you write the card twice: once from the Top-20 list in the chapter, once from memory to see what didn't stick. Print [my-5.pdf](my-5.pdf) after Chapter 9; you fill it in Chapter 10.

The book's finish line: "The card isn't done when it's written; it's done when you can reproduce it blind."

## What goes in your five

Your misses from the mock, plus anything from the r/ClaudeAI failure threads that surprised you, as Chapter 9 suggests. The bottom of my-5.md shows the book's own worked example of one miss turned into one line.
