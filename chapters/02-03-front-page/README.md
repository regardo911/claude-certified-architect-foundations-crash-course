# Chapters 2 and 3: the front page

Page 1 of your cram sheet. The top is Chapter 2's facts card, scoring rule and domain-weight map. Under the weight map comes the ranked attack order from Appendix A, then Chapter 3's pattern lens and the six scenario shapes.

Print one copy of [front-page.pdf](front-page.pdf) (or open [front-page.md](front-page.md)). Fill the Chapter 2 half on Friday night; come back for the pattern half once you've finished Chapter 3.

You're done when you pass Chapter 2's checkpoint: "Cover the page. Can you state the format, the clock, the cost, the scoring rule, and where the points live, from memory?" And Chapter 3's: "four or five" of its five scenarios right, pattern and move.

For your own exam, the weight map is the part that earns its keep: it tells you where to spend the hours you have left before you sit. The attack order underneath it says which domain gets them first.
