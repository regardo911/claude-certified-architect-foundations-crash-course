# Your last 24 hours, in order

From Appendix A. If you're reading this the day before, here's the whole book compressed to a checklist:

- [ ] 1. Confirm the exam actually opens for you (the partner-network access, the prerequisite modules) so you're not scrambling for access under pressure.
- [ ] 2. Finish your one-page cram sheet and your Top-20 card, in ranked attack order.
- [ ] 3. Take the Chapter 10 mock under a real clock if you haven't, and write your "My 5" from the misses.
- [ ] 4. Render an honest verdict: comfortable clear with no red domain means go; a lit-up domain means rebuild that block first, even if it costs a day.
- [ ] 5. Set up the room tonight: clear desk, single monitor, quiet space, ID ready, tech checked.
- [ ] 6. Tomorrow: normal breakfast, block two and a half hours, check in fifteen minutes early, skim the Top-20 last, then put it away.
- [ ] 7. Run the clock plan for whichever interface you get, and never leave a blank.
